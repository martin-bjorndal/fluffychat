# Recordings

`kortharkatt.wav` is cut from "Weibliche Britisch Kurzhaar will Futter C1277
MIAUEN.wav" (a female British Shorthair, born 2009, asking for food) by
PantheraLeo1359531, on Wikimedia Commons:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Weibliche_Britisch_Kurzhaar_will_Futter_C1277_MIAUEN.wav

Licence: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
(`LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt`). Changes: the five meows (at 0.25–1.45,
2.2–3.95, 4.95–6.3, 7.25–8.85 and 10.85–12.15 s of the original) were cut
out with 20 ms fades and a quarter of a second of silence between them,
mixed down to mono, resampled from 48 kHz to 22.05 kHz (averaging) and
normalised.

`scripts/gruppechat_sounds.py` makes the notification sounds "Mjau" and
"Kattunge" and the "Katter" effect from it (`MEOWS` there gives where each
meow is in this file); "Kattunge" and two meows of the effect are played
faster, which makes them higher. The app credits the recording on its
licences page (`gruppechatRegisterSoundLicenses` in
`lib/gruppechat/sounds.dart`).
